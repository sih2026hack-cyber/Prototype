ARGUS

One extension, two checks, both answered by your ARGUS server. The AI keys stay on the server.

Your comments

Comments on YouTube, Reddit and Instagram are checked when you press Post.

Off by default. Messages are checked but never saved, and never shown to a moderator.

Images in your feed

Hover over an image on Instagram, Reddit or YouTube and click Check, or right-click any image on any site.

Off by default: every check sends the image to your server and uses Meta API credits. When the server's credits run out, images say "Couldn't check" instead.

Your history

When you send several harsh messages to the same person (or comments on the same post) in a week, ARGUS tells you, so you can notice the pattern. Only you see this. It stays in this browser: who it was to is scrambled, never the message itself, and it's erased after 30 days.
